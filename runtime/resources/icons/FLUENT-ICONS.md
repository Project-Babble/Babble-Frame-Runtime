The `fluent_*.svg` files preserve the icon geometry used in Baballonia's
`src/Baballonia/Assets/Icons.axaml`. The accompanying 40 × 40 PNGs are transparent
white masks rendered from those paths; the debug dashboard applies its own
foreground color. Source SVGs remain available for regeneration or resizing.

The matching Baballonia resource keys are `EmojiLaughRegular`,
`EyeTrackingRegular`, `TopSpeedRegular`, `CameraRegular`, `SendLoggingRegular`,
`DocumentCopyRegular`, `SettingsRegular`, `HomeRegular`, and `DataFunnelRegular`
(`fluent_capture`, the dataset capture row).
